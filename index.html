Privacy Policy

App: Yalla Shoot (Android package com.livetvsx.app)
Effective date: September 27, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yalla Shoot (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application. Yalla Shoot is a football (soccer) live-score app that shows live matches, match details, lineups, head-to-head history, and lets you follow favorite matches and leagues and receive goal and match-status notifications.

Summary: We do not require an account, we do not collect your name, email or phone number, we do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell your data. Your favorites are stored only on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The App has no sign-up or login, and we operate no server that stores user data.

The following information exists only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Favorites: the matches and leagues you choose to follow.
  • Notification records: a small list of match events already notified, so the same goal or status change is not alerted twice.
  • App preferences such as display settings.

This data is kept in the App’s private on-device storage. It is removed when you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.

2. How the App Uses the Internet

To display live scores, the App sends requests directly from your device to a third-party sports data provider, API-Football (api-sports.io). These requests ask for football data (fixtures, scores, lineups, events, head-to-head). Team and league logos are loaded from the image addresses supplied by that data.

As with any internet request, the provider and its hosting infrastructure may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, device/network information, and the request time. We do not add your name or any account identifier to these requests. The provider’s handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy: api-football.com/privacy.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications (Android 13+ asks for permission), the App shows local notifications for goals and match status changes (kick-off, half-time, full-time) of the matches and leagues you follow. These are generated on your device by checking live data; they are not sent through advertising or marketing services. To deliver alerts when the App is not open, the App uses Android background tasks, which the operating system schedules and may delay. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

5. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking technologies. We do not build user profiles and we do not use your data for advertising.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it with third parties, except that your device communicates with the sports data provider described in Section 2 in order for the App to function. We may disclose information only if required by law, which is unlikely since we hold none.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all App data lives on your device, you control it. To delete it, remove individual favorites inside the App, clear the App’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Yalla Shoot → Storage, or uninstall the App. We hold no server-side copy that needs to be deleted.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). It does not include social features, accounts, or ads. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help resolve it.

9. Security

Requests to the data provider are made over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored on your device is protected by Android’s app sandboxing. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but since we do not collect personal data, the risk is minimal.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. As we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete on our side; you can remove all locally stored data yourself as described in Section 7. You may also contact us with any privacy question.

11. Third-Party Content and Disclaimer

Match data, team names, and logos are provided by third parties and belong to their respective owners. Yalla Shoot is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any football league, club, federation, or broadcaster. The App does not host or stream video content.
